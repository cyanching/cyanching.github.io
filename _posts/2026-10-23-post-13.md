Sometimes, you just want to stare at a colourful, mesmerising animation of fluid dynamics, interacting with it only every now and then as you zone out and empty your mind. Here is a link to an interactive, simplified 2D fluid dynamics simulation implemented by software engineer Raoul Harel at the Barcelona Supercomputing Centre, based on the 'Real-Time Fluid Dynamics for Games' paper by Jos Stam. On his webpage, you can find this very accessible paper for non-physicists, as well as instructions on possible interactions with the simulator.

[Access the simulation here](https://www.rharel.com/fluid-simulation/)
